1. Overview

a. Updates

  • Oct 8, 2026: Leads now show the customer's preferred contact time.
  • Sep 30, 2026: Added the steps for the Messages tab.

b. Key points

  • Pros get leads that match their services and travel area.
  • Reply to new leads within 2412 hours to keep a strong response rate.
  • Leads older than 30 days are archived automatically.
  • Customers can message more than one pro.

2. How it works

  1. The pro opens the Jobs tab in the app.
  2. They tap a lead to see the project details.
  3. They check the customer's preferred contact time.
  4. They send a quote or a message.

4. Troubleshooting

▶ The pro doesn't see new leads

Check that their targeting is on and their budget isn't used up. If both look right, ask them to update the app.

▶ A lead was charged twice

Open a case for the billing team and include the lead ID.
