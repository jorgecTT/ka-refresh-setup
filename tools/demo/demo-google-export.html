2. Getting started

  1. Create the account with an email or phone number.
  2. Add the services they offer.
  • Pick the main category first.
  • Add up to 10 related services.
  1. Set the travel area.
  2. Add a payment method.
  3. Turn on notifications so no lead is missed.

3. Profile and reviews

A complete profile gets more leads. Pros should add:

  • A clear profile photo or logo.
  • An introduction with their experience.
  • Photos of past work.
  • A fax number.
  • Business hours.

▶ How reviews work

Customers can leave a review after the job. The pro can't edit a review.

  • Pros can reply to each review once.
  • Pros can ask past customers for reviews from the Profile tab.

The weekly budget is the most a pro spends on leads in a week. It resets every SundayMonday.

▶ Example

A pro with a $100 weekly budget who gets 5 leads at $20 reaches the budget.

After that, they stop getting new leads until the budget resets.

8. Top Pro and badges

Top Pro is a badge for pros with great reviews and fast replies. It is checked every quarter.

  • At least 4.8 stars
  • At least 10 reviews
  • Replies to most leads
  • Has a website

9. Account safety

  • Never share the verification code.
  • Thumbtack will never ask for a password by phone.
  • Turn on two-step verification in Settings.

If a pro thinks their account was hacked, escalate to Trust & Safety right away.

10. Troubleshooting

▶ The pro doesn't see new leads

Check that targeting is on and the budget isn't used up. If both look right, ask them to update the app.

▶ A lead was charged twice

Open a case for the billing team and include the lead ID.

▶ The pro can't log in

  1. Check the email on the account.
  2. Send a password reset link.
  3. If it still fails, ask them to clear the app cache.

12. Pausing the account

Pros can pause their account when they are busy or on vacation. Leads stop until they turn it back on.

  1. Go to Settings.
  2. Tap Pause account.
  3. Pick an end date (optional).
